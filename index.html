Research

A few problems I have been thinking about and working on.

Speech & Low-Resource Dialects

Speech Transcription for the Sichuan–Chongqing Dialect

I designed a high-accuracy speech transcription system using Paraformer-large as the base model, built on a ~29,000-utterance subset of the KeSpeech dataset. To handle limited dialect data, I proposed a partial-parameter-freezing fine-tuning strategy that freezes low-level acoustic modules and fine-tunes only high-level semantic layers.

This reduced the character error rate from 9.5% to 7.05% on KeSpeech, and from 18.2% to 7.6% on the cross-corpus WSC-Eval set. Adding Qwen2-1.5B-Instruct as a post-correction module further lowered CER to 6.47%, and I deployed an interactive demo with FastAPI and Gradio that runs with a real-time factor below 0.02 on an RTX 4090.

Trust & Safety · Project Leader · 03/2025 – 03/2026

Real-Time Detection of Illegal Content in Livestreams

I led the development of a system that combines speech-to-text transcription with a BERT-based classification model to detect inappropriate business practices such as false advertising and exaggerated claims in e-commerce livestreams.

To address scarce training data, I tested multiple data augmentation strategies and applied domain-specific fine-tuning to improve both accuracy and robustness.

Vision & Robotics · Robot Software Development · 07/2024 – 08/2024

Vision-Guided Robotic Box-Handling

I contributed to a computer-vision-based robotic system for object recognition and automated box-handling on an Atlas development board, and developed the communication module that connects the vision-recognition module with the robot controller.
